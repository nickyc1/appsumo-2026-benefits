Open enrollment meeting recap

A plain-English summary of the 2026 benefits rollout — plus the full transcript if you want the details. Hosted by Trina Mayo-Day (NFP) with Megan Farnsworth and Angelina Olmos.

The 30-second version

Blue Cross's renewal came back with a ~50% increase, so for 2026 AppSumo moved carriers: medical → UnitedHealthcare, and dental, vision & all other benefits → Unum (HSA stays with isolved). AppSumo absorbed the cost increase, so your rates are flat-to-slightly-lower. There are three medical plans, generous HSA money, and a lot of new voluntary options. You must make your elections in Insperity by Friday, Aug 7 — this is an active enrollment, so no elections means no coverage on Sept 1.

Dates & how to enroll
  • Plan year: Sept 1, 2026 – Aug 31, 2027.
  • Open enrollment: now through Friday, Aug 7. Active enrollment — if you don't elect, you have no benefits on Sept 1.
  • Enroll in Insperity with your usual login.
  • Eligible: regular full-time W2 teammates. Dependents: legal spouse/domestic partner (per CA) and children to age 26.
  • Mid-year changes only via a qualifying life event (birth, marriage, loss of coverage) — tell HR within 30 days.
Medical — 3 UnitedHealthcare plans
  • HDHP (HSA-eligible, UHC Choice network), Base PPO and Buy Up PPO (both UHC Choice+). All cover 100% preventive care.
  • The HDHP mirrors your old BCBS high-deductible plan: $5,000 / $10,000 deductible that equals the out-of-pocket max, with 0% coinsurance (100% covered after the deductible).
  • Embedded deductible: if one family member hits their $5,000 individual deductible, their care is 100% covered for the rest of the year — even before the family $10,000 is met.
  • Deductible is calendar-year. Your BCBS deductible credit transfers to UHC (requested 9/2; takes 60–90 days to appear).
  • AppSumo HSA gift: $1,200 (you) · $1,800 (+spouse) · $2,100 (+kids) · $2,400 (family) — about double the market benchmark.
  • 2026 HSA max (AppSumo's money + yours): $4,400 self-only / $8,750 family. Triple tax-advantaged.
  • New on all plans: a doula allowance and chiropractic care.
⚠️ During the 60–90 day deductible-transfer window, don't pay a doctor's bill right away. Your credit is coming — the provider re-runs the claim so it adjudicates correctly.
Prescriptions
  • Pharmacy is through OptumRx — check the formulary for anything you take regularly.
  • To save, also use GoodRx and the $4 generic lists at Walmart / grocery pharmacies (these run outside your insurance).
Dental (Unum)
  • $1,500/yr per covered person.
  • Preventive (cleanings, exams, X-rays) 100%.
  • Basic 80%, major 50% after a $50 / $150 deductible.
  • Ortho: $1,500 lifetime (adult & child).
Vision (Unum)
  • 12 / 12 / 24 plan (exam & lenses yearly, frames every 12–24 mo).
  • $130 frame allowance + 20% off any overage.
  • Glasses or contacts — not both.
Life & disability (Unum)
  • Basic life & AD&D: $50,000, 100% employer-paid. Review your beneficiaries.
  • Voluntary life: up to $300k (you) / $100k (spouse) / $10k (child). Because it's the first Unum year, you can take up to $100,000 with no medical questions.
  • Short-term disability: 60% of income up to $1,000/wk for 25 weeks. Must elect during OE or face a one-year wait. AppSumo salary continuation can offset it (they don't stack) — important for maternity leave.
  • Long-term disability: 60% of monthly income up to $6,000/mo, starting when STD ends.
Extra protection (Unum — replaces the old Aflac)
  • Accident: cash per injury (ER, ambulance, X-rays, PT…). Average payout ~$2,100–$2,400.
  • Critical illness: $10k–$30k lump sum on a major diagnosis (heart attack, stroke, cancer…).
  • Hospital indemnity: $1,500 on admission + $100/day ($3,000 ICU + $200/day). Pays only if admitted.
Perks & programs
  • EAP (Health Advocate): counseling, virtual therapy, a medical bill saver, 24/7 clinicians — free.
  • Assist America travel assistance worldwide — free.
  • Pet insurance (Wishbone) and Insure Choice marketplace (auto / home / renters / legal) — optional, direct-bill.
