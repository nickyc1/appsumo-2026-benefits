Your action list
Every election — and what we'd pick

Open enrollment is active. You must make these choices in Insperity by Friday, Aug 7 or you'll have no benefits on Sept 1. Here's every decision in order, each with a plain recommendation. These are general suggestions — your call depends on your situation.

1
Medical plan Must decide
UHC Choice HDHP · Choice+ PPO (Base) · Choice+ PPO Buy Up — pick one.
Our pick: For most people, the HDHP — lowest premiums, $1,200–$2,400 of free HSA money, and it came out cheapest in every scenario HR modeled. Use the finder at the top of this page for your own situation. Choose the Buy Up only if you want fixed copays and can't front a deductible.
👶 Growing your family? Even with a delivery, the HDHP still wins if you have savings for upfront costs. New for 2026: every plan includes a doula allowance and chiropractic care.
2
HSA contribution Decide if HDHP
Through isolved — only relevant if you choose the HDHP. Triple tax-free (in, grows, and out for medical/dental/vision).
Our pick: Contribute at least enough per paycheck (pre-tax) to cover your deductible, so a big bill never stings. The 2026 max — your money + AppSumo's — is $4,400 self-only / $8,750 family. Even $50–$100 a paycheck compounds tax-free and is yours to keep.
3
Dental (Unum) Recommended
$1,500/yr per person · cleanings, exams & X-rays 100% free · basic 80% · major 50% · ortho $1,500 lifetime.
Our pick: Enroll. Preventive care is free and premiums are low — it pays for itself at two cleanings a year. Match the coverage tier (you / +spouse / +kids / family) to your medical.
4
Vision (Unum) Recommended if you wear glasses
Exam + lenses yearly, frames every 12/24 months, $130 frame allowance (+20% off any overage). Glasses or contacts — not both.
Our pick: Enroll if you or a dependent wears glasses or contacts — the premium is a few dollars and usually less than one exam plus lenses. Skip only if no one in the household needs corrective lenses.
5
Basic Life & AD&D (Unum) Automatic · just review
$50,000, 100% paid by AppSumo — you're already covered, nothing to buy.
Our pick: Nothing to elect, but log in and name (or update) your beneficiaries. Don't skip this one — it's the step people forget.
6
Voluntary Life & AD&D (Unum) Consider — this year only
Up to $300k for you, $100k spouse, $10k child, on top of the free $50k.
Our pick: If anyone depends on your income, elect up to the $100,000 guaranteed-issue now — no medical questions are asked in this first Unum year, a window that won't come back. Later increases can require underwriting.
👶 Starting or growing a family? This is the easiest year you'll ever get to lock in extra life coverage with zero health questions.
7
Short-Term Disability (Unum) Recommended · now or never
Pays 60% of your income up to $1,000/week for up to 25 weeks if you're too sick or injured to work.
Our pick: Elect it during open enrollment. If you skip it now, there's a one-year waiting period before you can get it later. Note: AppSumo may continue your salary during leave, which can offset STD (they don't stack on top of each other).
👶 Any chance of maternity leave in the next year? This is the one to get in — the one-year waiting period makes it now-or-never.
8
Long-Term Disability (Unum) Recommended
Picks up where STD ends — 60% of monthly income up to $6,000/month, potentially to age 65.
Our pick: Elect it — inexpensive income protection for a serious injury or illness. Pair it with STD so the waiting period is already covered.
9
Accident coverage (Unum) Consider
Cash payouts per injury — ER, ambulance, X-rays, PT, etc. Average payout ~$2,100–$2,400. Replaces the old Aflac plan.
Our pick: Worth it if you have kids in sports or an active, accident-prone household. Pairs nicely with the HDHP — the cash helps cover your deductible.
10
Critical Illness (Unum) Optional
Lump sum of $10k–$30k if you're diagnosed with a major illness (heart attack, stroke, cancer, kidney/organ failure…).
Our pick: Consider it if you have a family history of the covered conditions or want extra cushion against a big diagnosis. Otherwise skippable.
11
Hospital Indemnity (Unum) Consider
Cash for a hospital stay: $1,500 on admission + $100/day ($3,000 for ICU + $200/day). Pays only if you're admitted.
Our pick: A strong add-on if a hospital stay is likely this year.
👶 A childbirth is a hospital admission — this pays cash on delivery that directly offsets your HDHP deductible. If you're expecting, this one can pay for itself.
12
Free programs Included · good to know
EAP (Health Advocate — counseling, virtual therapy, a medical bill saver, 24/7 clinicians) and Assist America travel assistance worldwide. Both free, no election.
Our pick: Nothing to elect — just save the numbers from the benefits site so you have them when you need them.
13
Optional extras Your call
Pet insurance (Wishbone, 70–80% reimbursement) and Insure Choice — a marketplace for auto, home, renters, legal and travel coverage.
Our pick: Nice-to-haves — buy only if you need them. These are direct-bill, not payroll-deducted, so they won't touch your paycheck.
